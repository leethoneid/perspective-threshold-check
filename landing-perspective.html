Perspective API shuts down Dec 31. Will your moderation silently pass everything on Jan 1?

A self-run CLI that scans your call sites, replays your real traffic against a replacement scorer, and hands you a threshold-mapping report — before the shutdown, not after.

The problem

Google's Perspective API stops working after December 31, 2026. No extension. No official migration support.

The dangerous failure isn't "it breaks loudly." In the Discourse plugin's code, when a response comes back without attributeScores — the shape of an error body — scores silently become 0.0. Every post is treated as clean. (Connection failures raise a visible error; error-body responses don't. What the endpoint actually returns on Jan 1 is still unknown — which is exactly why you check now, not then.)

And replacement scores aren't comparable. There is no single equivalent for TOXICITY; thresholds don't carry over from one scorer to another. The only way to set new thresholds is to replay your own traffic and see how the new scorer behaves on it.

What this does (self-run CLI, runs in your own infra)

  1. Call-site scan — finds every Perspective call in your code, and every place a missing score becomes 0.
  2. Shadow dual-run — runs Perspective and your replacement side by side on your real traffic, before Dec 31, and records how each scores the same comments.
  3. Threshold-mapping report — a measurement report: old vs. new scorer behavior on your traffic, plus a threshold map you set yourself.

What this is not

Not a hosted service. Not a moderation model. Not a guarantee. It measures; you decide the thresholds; you own the moderation outcomes. It never sends your comment data anywhere — it runs where your code already runs.
